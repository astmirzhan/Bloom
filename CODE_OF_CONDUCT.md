# Code of conduct

Bloom follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@astmirzhan](https://github.com/astmirzhan),
privately. Reports are kept confidential.
